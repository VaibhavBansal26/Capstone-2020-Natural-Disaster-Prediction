    {# ------------------------------------------------------------------ HOW IT WORKS ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}

    How it works

    1. 1

      Train once

      ndp-fetch-data downloads the public datasets; ndp-train fits every model and saves it with its metrics and plots.

    2. 2

      Predict from artifacts

      Requests only load saved models and score your inputs. Nothing trains or downloads per request.

    3. 3

      Estimates, not forecasts

      Each number is a regression over past events, shown with its accuracy. Use it to explore the data, never as a warning system.
