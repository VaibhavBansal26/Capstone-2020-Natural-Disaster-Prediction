Natural Disaster Prediction

A set of small machine-learning models, each trained once on a public historical dataset, that turn a handful of inputs (a location, a date, a rainfall total) into a risk estimate: earthquake magnitude, tsunami magnitude, river level, hurricane wind, storm class. Alongside them: a live weather check, an alerting helper that finds hospitals and police stations near a place, social-media sentiment versus monetary impact, and a side-by-side comparison of four regression models.

Originally submitted in 2020 as a Flask + Bootstrap project; rebuilt on FastAPI, HTMX and Leaflet with the training code separated from the web layer. Outputs are statistical estimates, not operational forecasts or warnings.

How it works

1

Train once

ndp-fetch-data downloads the public CSVs and ndp-train fits every model, saving artifacts, metrics and plots to disk.

2

Predict from artifacts

Web requests only load saved models and score your inputs. Nothing trains, downloads or writes files per request, so pages answer in milliseconds.

3

Estimates, not forecasts

Each number is a regression over past events with its accuracy shown next to it. Use it to explore the data, never as a warning system.
