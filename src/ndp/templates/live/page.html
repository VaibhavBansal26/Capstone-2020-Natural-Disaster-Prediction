Live hazards

What is happening now, from public data feeds: the UN/EC disaster alert system, a weather model for a place you choose, and an optional watcher that can alert your contacts about a real earthquake. This page reports other organisations' data. It is not an official warning service; follow your local authorities.

Global alerts (GDACS)

Earthquakes, tropical cyclones, floods, volcanoes, droughts and wildfires of the last 30 days from the Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System. Red, Orange and Green are GDACS's own assessment of likely humanitarian impact, not this app's.
