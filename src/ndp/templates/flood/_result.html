Persistence is the simplest possible forecast: the level stays where it is now. A useful model has to beat it, so it is shown next to every forecast; the model card shows how often it does on held-out data. "Rising" and "Falling" mean the forecast differs from the current level by at least 10 cm.

Read this before relying on it

  • The {{ fmt(threshold) }} m reference level comes from the original 2020 project. On this gauge the level has drifted upward over the years and sat above it for most of 2017 and 2018, so being above it does not by itself signal a flood.
  • The model improves only modestly on "no change" and mostly misses sudden rises. Held-out recall for rises of 10 cm or more within 3 hours is under 10%. See the model card.
  • It covers one gauge only (Hoppers Crossing, Werribee River, Melbourne). For flood warnings use your local authority.
