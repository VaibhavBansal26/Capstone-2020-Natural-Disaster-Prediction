What this means

Within {{ fmt(result.radius_km, 0) }} km, significant earthquakes happened at the rate above during 1965 to 2016. If that rate stayed the same, the table gives the chance of at least one earthquake of each size in the next 1 or 10 years.

What it does not mean

It is not a prediction of when or whether a quake will strike, and no earthquake can be predicted. Few or no past events is not proof of safety: faults can be quiet for far longer than 50 years. The M 8 row is extrapolated from the magnitude mix and tends to run high: worldwide the catalogue holds 40 events of M 8 or more where the same method implies 72 (at M 7 the fit is close). Prepare for the hazard whatever the number.
