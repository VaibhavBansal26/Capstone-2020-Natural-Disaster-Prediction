Earthquake hazard

How often have significant earthquakes struck around a place? Pick a point and a radius; the page counts the historical events (USGS significant-earthquakes catalogue, 1965 to 2016) and turns their frequency into the chance of a magnitude 6, 7 or 8 earthquake over 1 and 10 years. This is a long-term rate, not a prediction of when the next earthquake will happen.
